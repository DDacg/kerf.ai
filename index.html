FAQ

Questions

Do I need to know how to edit?

No. You describe what you want in plain English, or tick the quick options. Kerf AI does the cutting, captions, zooms and graphics.

What can I upload?

MP4 and MOV videos up to 2 GB, and JPG, PNG or HEIC photos. Phone footage, camera footage, screen recordings and AI-generated clips all work.

What if I don't like the result?

Reply in the chat with what to change, like "make the captions bigger" or "remove the music". A round of changes costs 5 credits.

Do unused credits carry over?

On Creator and Studio, unused monthly credits roll over for one month. Credits you buy as top-ups stay while you have an active plan.

Who owns the videos?

You do. Everything you upload and everything Kerf AI makes for you is yours to post, sell or use in ads.

Can I try it before paying?

You can open the editor and look around for free. Sending requests needs a plan, so every edit gets the processing power it needs.
