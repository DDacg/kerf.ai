Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026

Kerf AI ("Kerf AI", "we", "us") is an online editor that edits videos and photos based on what you ask for in a chat. This policy explains what information we collect when you use kerf-ai-virid.vercel.app and any future Kerf AI domain (the "Service"), how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your email address, and if you sign in with Google or Apple, the name and profile photo that service shares with us.
  • Your files and messages: the videos, photos and other files you upload, the edit requests you type, and the edited results we create for you.
  • Payment details: when you buy a plan or credits, payment is handled by Stripe. We receive your plan, purchase amounts and a Stripe customer ID. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Messages to us: anything you send when you contact support.

Information collected automatically

  • Sign-in data: a secure session saved in your browser so you stay signed in.
  • Basic technical data: IP address, browser type, device type and pages visited, recorded by our hosting and sign-in providers to keep the Service running and secure.
  • Usage data: your credit balance and the edits you run, so we can charge credits correctly.

We do not use advertising trackers, and we do not sell your personal information.

How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and keep you signed in.
  • To edit your files as you ask, and to save your chats and results so you can come back to them.
  • To process payments, add credits and manage your subscription.
  • To answer support requests and send important messages about your account (for example sign-in links and receipts).
  • To prevent abuse, fraud and illegal use, and to keep the Service secure.
  • To fix problems and improve the Service.

We do not use your uploaded files or edits to train AI models.

AI processing

To carry out your edits, your requests and files are sent to AI service providers (including Anthropic, the maker of Claude) that process them on our behalf. These providers may only use your content to provide the service to us, under their commercial terms, and not to train their models.

Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers that help us run Kerf AI, and only as needed:

  • Supabase for accounts, sign-in and storing your chats.
  • Vercel for hosting the website.
  • Stripe for payments.
  • Google and Apple if you choose to sign in with them.
  • AI and media processing providers (such as Anthropic) to perform your edits.
  • Email providers to send sign-in links and account emails.

We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect the rights and safety of our users or others, or as part of a sale or merger of the business, in which case this policy will continue to apply.

How long we keep information

We keep your account information while your account is open. Uploaded files and edited results are kept so you can download them and are deleted when you delete them, close your account, or after 90 days of account inactivity, whichever comes first. We keep payment records as long as tax and accounting laws require.

Your choices and rights

  • Access, correct or delete: you can ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account and data by emailing us.
  • Payments: you can cancel your subscription at any time.
  • Emails: we only send account and service emails. If we ever send marketing emails, each one will have an unsubscribe link.

Depending on where you live (for example California or the European Union), you may have additional rights under local law. We will honor those requests as the law requires.

Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls that keep each account's data private to that account, and trusted providers. No online service is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

Kerf AI is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page, and for important changes we will let you know by email or in the app.
